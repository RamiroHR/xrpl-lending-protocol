Example campaign

Imagine the broker opens a vault dedicated to credit for quantum startups. Investors who want that theme subscribe to this vault; a later climate or biotech raise is a new vault with new dates. The theme is the mandate and underwriting story — on-chain, the vault enforces phases and credentials, not the borrower’s industry label.

The problem

Lending money to companies is still hard to trust and hard to exit

Private credit means investors lend to companies outside the public stock market — often through a fund. That idea is fine. The plumbing is the problem.

Who is allowed in?

Today that answer often lives in emails and spreadsheets. You cannot easily check on a shared system whether someone is approved to invest. It depends on who you know.

Your money is stuck

Once you put capital in, you usually wait until the fund ends. Selling your piece early to another approved investor is difficult or impossible.

Paperwork and bank wires

Putting money in, paying interest, and getting money back still means bank transfers and back-office matching — slow, easy to disagree about, hard to verify.

What we are not claiming

We are not saying “anyone can invest.” Checks like KYC (know-your-customer identity checks) still matter. We want those rules to be visible and enforced on the ledger, not only in a private database.

The solution

One vault = one themed funding campaign

Broker picks a mandate (e.g. quantum credit), opens a closed-ended vault with a fixed calendar, and invites KYC’d LPs. Credentialed investors subscribe in XRP, hold MPT shares, and earn via Price Per Share. The loan is underwritten to that mandate — then settled on-ledger.

1. On-chain KYC ring

Permissioned Domain + Credentials. Uncredentialed deposits fail at the vault — tecNO_AUTH.

2. Tradeable shares

During locked Investment, credentialed holders can transfer MPTs. Unauthorized recipients are rejected.

3. Verifiable yield

PPS rises when cash enters without minting shares (coupon / repayment). Cash-basis V1.1 — no off-chain yield book.

What the ledger does not store

The industry theme is product narrative and broker underwriting. On-chain enforcement is phases, credentials, and share transfers — not a “quantum” tag on the borrower.

How it works

Three protocol-enforced phases

Wrong-phase transactions are rejected by the ledger. Click a phase (or use the demo scripts in order).

Subscription

  • Credentialed investors call VaultDeposit.
  • MPT shares mint at current PPS.
  • Uncredentialed attempts are rejected.
  • Deposits stop at SubscriptionDate.

Investment

  • Deposits and withdrawals are locked.
  • Broker + borrower co-sign LoanSet; principal draws down.
  • PPS rises when repayment arrives — cash-basis, no off-chain yield book.
  • Coupon injection (tfVaultDonation) probed — absent from devnet 3.4.0-rc5 · DX-13.
  • MPT transfers stay inside the KYC ring.

Redemption

  • Borrower repayment lands; PPS can rise (cash-basis).
  • After RedemptionDate, investors call VaultWithdraw.
  • MPT shares are burned.
  • XRP returns at current PPS.

Developer feedback

Key friction points — with proposed fixes

Drawn from the live build log. Full write-up in docs/DEVEX_REPORT.md.

High · SDK bug

DX-06 · Wrong LoanSet counterparty prefix

signLoanSetByCounterparty still uses STX signing after fixCleanup3_4_0. Needs CPT (encodeForSigningCounterparty). Blocks every dual-party loan on Devnet.

Fix: one-line SDK swap + tutorial note until patched.

High · API confusion

DX-12 · GracePeriod is not “grace”

Window is [Due − Grace, Due) — opens early, does not extend past deadline. Name implies the opposite → tecEXPIRED traps.

Fix: rename / diagram in XLS-66 docs; include deadline in error metadata.

High · Doc gap + SDK

DX-13 · tfVaultDonation absent from SDK, codec & devnet

Coupon injection (deposit without minting shares → PPS rise) is a V1.1 Loaded feature. Official docs say ”VaultDeposit has no flags.” Probing 0x00010000 → temINVALID_FLAG on 3.4.0-rc5.

Fix: export tfVaultDonation in xrpl.js; add to binary-codec; document in VaultDeposit reference.
